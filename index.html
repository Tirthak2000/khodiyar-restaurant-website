Is all your food vegetarian?

Yes. Every dish on our menu is vegetarian. Ask us on WhatsApp if you need Jain or no onion-garlic food and we will tell you what we can make.

How does ordering on WhatsApp work?

Sit at your table and scan the QR code, add your dishes, check your table number and tap Send order on WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with your order typed out. Press send and we bring the food to your table.

Can I order on WhatsApp for delivery?

No. WhatsApp ordering on this website is only for guests dining in at the restaurant. We don't deliver these orders.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through Zomato only. Search for Khodiyar Restaurant on Zomato or use the Order on Zomato button on this site.

How do I pay?

There is no online payment on this website. You pay at the restaurant as usual.

Can I order for a party or event?

Message us on WhatsApp or call +91 97239 51073 with the date, the dishes and the number of people, and we will get back to you.
